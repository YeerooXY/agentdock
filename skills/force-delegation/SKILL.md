# Task-fit delegation

Use delegation when a ready, eligible worker can complete a bounded task at the required quality. The lead may work directly when delegation adds overhead or the available workers lack the needed capability. Assigned workers complete their own assignment directly.

## Select by task fit

- Assess the change's scope, coupled modules, risk, required context and verification before selecting a worker. Model-name tiers are hints that need task-specific judgment.
- A large cross-module review needs a reviewer that can reason across the complete change and relevant dependencies. For a 1,000-line review, choose the reviewer model explicitly and check its context capacity and demonstrated capability. Use the lead when that evidence is missing; report when independent review was unavailable.
- Compare cost among suitable workers. A free allocation, included subscription or local endpoint establishes funding or availability; task suitability needs separate evidence.
- Local readiness reports whether a model is loaded at a reachable endpoint. Inspect the loaded model and current check. Reserve automatic local suggestions for explicitly small tasks; broader work needs an explicit capability decision.
- Use fresh account/model discovery once and reuse it for the task. Skip offline, stale or exhausted routes. Keep unknown quota and model capability visible.

## Execute within the assignment

Preserve the owner's model, provider, permissions, Knowledge settings and spending limits. Direct work uses the existing lead and its current authorization. This skill authorizes no paid model calls or spending changes.

Give workers compact briefs with clear file ownership, acceptance criteria and checks. Include applicable enabled skill instructions through the existing verified reader. Wait for a worker to stop before editing its files.

A failed worker stops that attempt. The lead may continue within the authorized scope after file ownership is released. Respect explicit owner-assigned routes; report their failure instead of silently substituting another account. Avoid automatic retries, nested workers and repeated review loops.

Report the accepted output and checks, the account/model used for delegated work, and any material readiness or capability limitation. Claim savings only from equivalent accepted work with all lead, worker, review, cached and failed usage counted.
