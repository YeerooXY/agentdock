# Shops and purchase flows

Optimize for selecting the correct item and understanding the purchase. More clicks or time on site can reflect confusion; measure completed tasks and purchase quality alongside business outcomes.

## Catalogue and comparison

Use categories and filters grounded in how people choose this product: size, compatibility, material, use, price and availability as relevant. Show applied filters, result context, clear removal and an empty-results recovery. Keep comparable card fields consistent. Preserve selections and scroll when returning from a product.

## Product decision

Show identity, meaningful images, selected variation, price and unit, stock/availability, quantity, delivery cost or its calculation, returns and relevant conditions. Use images for scale, finish, fit and detail. Label unavailable variants clearly; do not silently substitute another variant. Confirm exactly what was added to the cart.

Avoid relying on color swatches alone. Give variants text names and selected/unavailable states. A sticky purchase action should retain enough item/price/variant context and leave space for the on-screen keyboard and safe areas.

## Cart and checkout

Make quantities editable, removals recoverable where practical, and totals understandable. Disclose recurring charges, taxes, fees and commitments before purchase. Explain delivery choices with their actual costs and expectations. Offer guest purchase where the business supports it; justify any required account.

Use correct input types and autocomplete tokens. Permit paste and password managers. Keep valid entries after errors; link error summaries to fields where useful. Prevent duplicate order submission on the server and provide an accurate outcome/recovery path for uncertain payment results.

Never simulate working payment on a prototype. Label demo limitations visibly. Live payments, tax rules, refunds and consumer rights need the actual provider, jurisdiction and owner-approved facts. Don't fabricate a blanket refund exclusion or default marketing consent.

## Verification

Exercise search, filtering, empty results, variant selection, gallery, cart edits and checkout errors on phones, tablets and desktops. Check iOS and Android keyboards, address autofill, long product names and slower networks. Use authorized test modes; do not place real orders or submit real personal data for an audit.

Sources: [Baymard product-page research](https://baymard.com/research/ecommerce-product-pages), [checkout research](https://baymard.com/research/checkout-usability), [EU consumer information](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/index_en.htm). Findings are conditional; check the relevant market's current primary legal sources.
