# CTA reference register

Observed 31 October 2026. These are dated structural examples, not conversion results. Desktop Chromium and emulated iPhone WebKit captures were inspected; contact sheets were used for full-page order and original data for control details. Twelve first-click destinations were inspected in desktop Chromium; no transaction, account, booking or download was completed.

The study attempted 36 URLs and saved 142 initial PNGs. The capture script marked 33 sites ok and three partial. Visual review additionally excluded paper republic (redirected to Google); Canva was excluded for 403/unsupported-client responses. Thirty-four entries provide at least some intended-page evidence, often with overlays or missing media. Raw capture success is not design validity.

The public URLs below identify the sources. Local original captures, corrected per-site observations and first-click screenshots remain with the study deliverable; they are not distributed in this skill. Locate that deliverable through the project before citing a local image. Recheck a live page before making claims about what it currently shows.

## Inspection cautions

- Automated CTA scores chose social links, navigation toggles, cart icons and modal actions in several captures. Confirm the visitor task, visible control and destination manually.
- An exact-label repeat can lead to different plans, models or services; inspect each destination. A persistent control counts once.
- Consent and newsletter overlays materially changed first-screen visibility. DOM presence and CSS coordinates do not prove an unobscured, usable action.
- The iPhone Obsidian capture showed a macOS headline download. The Todoist capture offered an iPhone route. Treat platform detection as behaviour to verify.
- Price, stock, review counts and promotion truth were not audited. No causal conversion claim follows from these examples.
- Mac desktop, Android and physical-device checks were not part of this reference capture. A product built from it still needs the full verification workflow.
