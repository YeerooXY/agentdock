# Research provenance and references

Revision: 23 October 2026. This library is an original, compact synthesis of the full [handbook](handbook.md), [reference atlas](atlas.md), [verification workbook](workbook.md) and [source register](source-register.md) in this skill.

The October 23 study inspected ten URLs from nine organizations: Linear, Stripe, Basecamp, GOV.UK, Apple, IKEA (category and product), Patagonia, Notion and Mailchimp. It recorded 36 initial page/profile combinations and eight additional IKEA captures after rejecting optional cookies. Selected sites received two tablet profiles. Patagonia showed a desktop interstitial. These are observations from that session, not current assurances or conversion experiments.

The study read four Apple video transcripts and inspected frame samples from three. Full transcripts, screenshots and working logs are retained with the research deliverables rather than inserted into every agent context. No competitor account, purchase, security audit or real-device performance study was conducted. No physical Apple/Android device coverage was established by those emulations.

## Primary standards and platform documentation

- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) and [quick reference](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/): check criterion scope and exceptions.
- [WAI modal-dialog pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/): behavior, keyboard and focus.
- [Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals), [font practices](https://web.dev/articles/font-best-practices), [animation performance](https://web.dev/articles/animations-guide): implementation and measurement.
- [MDN reduced motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/prefers-reduced-motion) and [pointer capabilities](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/pointer).
- [GOV.UK content design](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/content-design/writing-for-gov-uk) and [error summary](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/error-summary/).
- [Apple writing](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/writing), [Writing for interfaces, WWDC22](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/10037/), [The details of UI typography, WWDC20](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10175/).
- [European Commission data protection](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-individuals_en) and [EU consumer information](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/index_en.htm): jurisdiction-specific implementation needs actual business facts.
- [OWASP Secure Headers](https://owasp.org/www-project-secure-headers/).

## Published research and observed examples

- [NN/G homepage principles](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/), [scrolling and attention](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolling-and-attention/), [concise scannable writing](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/concise-scannable-and-objective-how-to-write-for-the-web/).
- [Baymard product pages](https://baymard.com/research/ecommerce-product-pages) and [checkout usability](https://baymard.com/research/checkout-usability).
- [Linear](https://linear.app/), [Stripe](https://stripe.com/), [Basecamp](https://basecamp.com/), [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/), [Apple MacBook Air](https://www.apple.com/macbook-air/), [IKEA](https://www.ikea.com/), [Patagonia](https://www.patagonia.com/), [Notion](https://www.notion.com/), [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/): inspect current pages before claiming what they show today.

Revalidate changing sources when applying numerical, legal, release or browser-support claims. Use primary sources for technical rules. Treat colors, font counts, section recipes and example compositions as working defaults unless a source establishes a narrower requirement.
